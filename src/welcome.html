Read Aloud is installed 🎉

Select text on any page and hear it in a natural voice.

  1. Pin it to your toolbar

    Click the puzzle-piece icon 🧩 at the top right of the browser, then click the pin 📌 next to Read Aloud. Its blue speaker icon now stays visible.

  2. Select text and right-click

    Highlight a sentence or paragraph, right-click it, and choose Read aloud.

  3. Give it a minute the first time

    The first time, it downloads the voice (about 330 MB, usually under a minute). After that it starts almost instantly and works offline.

Handy extras

  • Click the speaker icon to pick a voice, change the speed, or pause / stop.
  • Keyboard: Alt+Shift+R reads the selection, Alt+Shift+S stops.

Private by design

The voice runs on your own computer. Your text is never sent anywhere. The only download is the voice model itself, from Hugging Face. English only for now.
